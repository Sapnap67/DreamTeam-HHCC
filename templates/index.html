What this prototype does

YOLO detects people, bicycles, cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks, traffic lights, and stop signs. MediaPipe Pose estimates visible body landmarks for the selected pedestrian.

“Likely walking” uses short-term body movement. Head orientation is a low-confidence visual proxy; it cannot prove attention, awareness, intent, or that someone noticed the truck.

The crossing result is a conservative advisory from zone overlap, not a verified safety decision or production-ready technology.
